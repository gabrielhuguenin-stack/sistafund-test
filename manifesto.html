The manifesto

The game is changing

Gender equality is fueling discussions everywhere. General awareness is everything, and yes, it is happening.

We need more female founders

Gender-balanced teams deliver superior results: companies with more than 30% women founders are up to 2.5× more profitable. They are bold, they overcome obstacles and they challenge conventions.

The time has come for SISTAFUND

€70m mobilised as Europe's largest gender-lens venture fund, backing companies founded by women and gender-balanced teams across France, Europe and the United States, from inception through scaling.

We are a new generation of venture capital founders

Accomplished entrepreneurs, strategic partners and sector experts committed to disrupting traditional approaches, across HealthTech, Fintech, Sustainability and Frontier Tech.

Women don't need to be shown the way. They need to be given resources

Our mission is to tell new stories and create value by giving founders the capital and the support their vision requires.

Our legacy will be equality
