A new generation of venture capital

We back exceptional female founders and gender-balanced teams building the companies of tomorrow, from Pre-Seed to Seed, mainly across Europe.

Hands-on partners

As entrepreneurs ourselves, we know the energy and support needed to shift the paradigm. We work with our community of founders and advisors to help companies get further and faster, from the very beginning.

Driven by conviction

Companies with women in their founding teams perform better, up to 2.5× more profitable. We reveal founders who overcome adversity, shake up conventions and summon the future to challenge the present.

Our thesis

We lead Pre-Seed and Seed rounds with tickets from €700k to €3m, mainly across Europe with select opportunities in the US, backing teams with at least 30% of founders' equity held by women.
