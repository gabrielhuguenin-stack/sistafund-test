Questions,
answered

What is SISTAFUND?+

Launched in October 2022, SISTAFUND is a European venture fund with €70m under management, funding exceptional women founders and gender-balanced teams. It was initiated by Tatiana Jama and draws on the expertise of leading French entrepreneurs.

How much do you typically invest?+

We lead Pre-Seed and Seed rounds. Initial tickets typically range from ~€700k to €3m, with an average of €1.5m, depending on the stage and round dynamics, with reserved capacity for follow-ons.

What type of projects do you invest in?+

Companies founded or co-founded by women, mainly in France, the rest of Europe (including the UK) and the US, with a focus on HealthTech, Fintech, Sustainability and Frontier Tech, though we stay open to other sectors.

How do you ensure your criteria are met?+

We require at least 30% of founders' shares to be owned by women in the cap table at pre-seed stage, moving beyond tokenism to meaningful representation.

Can this criterion really influence performance?+

Yes. BCG research shows women-founded startups generate 10% more revenue over 5 years. For every dollar of funding, they generate 78 cents of revenue versus 31 cents for all-male teams.

What does your investment process look like?+

Every deal gets "4 eyes" from the internal team, then founders meet expert investor-partners for challenge sessions. Decisions go through our advisory and investment committees.

How do you work with your portfolio?+

Beyond board participation, we provide hands-on support in communications, PR & branding, public affairs and sales strategy, pursuing both financial and societal impact.

What are the links between SISTAFUND and SISTA?+

Both share the same vision and leadership through Tatiana Jama. SISTA NGO (est. 2018) works to reduce the funding gap for female entrepreneurs; SISTAFUND invests directly. A percentage of SISTAFUND's profits is allocated to SISTA NGO.

What is SUNSHINE?+

SUNSHINE is the management company behind SISTAFUND. Founded by Tatiana Jama and Isabelle Gallo, it merges entrepreneurial passion with VC expertise and is approved by the Autorité des Marchés Financiers.
